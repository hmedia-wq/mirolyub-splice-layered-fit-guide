MIROLYUB Apparel Fit Notes

Splice Core Layered Long Sleeve: One Garment, Two Sleeve Lines

MIROLYUB describes Splice Core as a single constructed layered garment. The short-sleeve overlay, long lower sleeves and contrasting lower edge are sewn into one top, so the look does not depend on buying or aligning two separate shirts. The official image shows the Army Green, Black and Dark Gray panels and dropped shoulder line.

The listing specifies a unisex cut, crew neckline, 100% cotton and a 230 gsm weight. Its size table gives flat chest widths from 64 cm in S to 72 cm in 2XL, with lengths from 69 cm to 77 cm. These are garment measurements, not body circumferences. Compare the chest width and overall length to a tee you own if you want the layered hem to sit in a specific place.
