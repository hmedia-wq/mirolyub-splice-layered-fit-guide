Where Will the Cuff Land on a Dropped-Shoulder Shirt?

Two shirts can have the same stated sleeve length and put the cuff in different places. On a dropped-shoulder top, the sleeve starts farther from the neck. Before buying, compare the distance from the middle of the back neck to the cuff on a shirt that already fits.

Measure a shirt you own

Lay your reference shirt flat, without stretching it. Measure from one shoulder seam to the other across the back. Then measure from one shoulder seam down the top edge of that sleeve to the cuff. Use centimetres for both. For a raglan or a shirt without a clear shoulder seam, measure directly from the centre back of the neck to the cuff and compare that measurement with the last column below.

Compare your shirt

For example, a reference shirt with a 58 cm shoulder and 55 cm sleeve measures approximately 84 cm from the centre line to the cuff. The M works out to 85.25 cm, about 1.25 cm longer. Decide whether that extra length is welcome at your wrist, then check the product’s separate chest and body-length measurements before choosing a size.
